Ein Azurblauer Traum

Year written: 2022

Ein Azurblauer Traum (English: An Azure Dream) is a 4000-word short story about growing up to become an adult.
We follow our protagonist through a bizarre fantasy world, until he slowly loses himself in a place he accidentally and involuntarily stepped into, calling him back more and more often.
With this story, I tried to catch growing up as something inevitable, while childhood becomes a dream, something mystical and precious. However, one most often only realizes that childhood is finite when it's already over.
