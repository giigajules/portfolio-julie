Locke, Fern & the Abandoned is a single-player puzzle game where you are in control of two characters simultaneously. Trapped in a city filled with abandoned book characters, you try to find a way back to your own story.

Description

For our third semester project, we had to create a digital game prototype incorporating the concept of "Illusion". Working in Unity, we spent the next six weeks building Locke, Fern & the Abandoned, a game about discarded book characters finding out about their fate, and trying everything in their power to escape it. While Locke is able to push and pull objects within a certain range, Fern can create two interlinked portals through which both the characters and objects can pass.
I took part in finding ideas, building the world and the story, and spent most of my time programming many of the game's crucial systems, including the push/pull and portal mechanic.
We left the project with a first prototype, including all main systems, two levels (one of which is a five-stage tutorial), and a story hook.
