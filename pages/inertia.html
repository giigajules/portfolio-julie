Inertia is a fast-paced 3D parkour endless runner set in an abstract neon cyberspace. Keep your momentum, combine slides, wallruns, and fluid moves to traverse the world, and survive as long as possible in a constantly shifting environment.

Description

As a freelancer, I filled the roles the solo-developer could not, honing my skills in different areas I did not have much experience in before. I designed the logo for exhibiting the game at Gamescom RAW Talent, and am currently working on a new UI.
Inertia is still in development and scheduled to release in late 2027.
