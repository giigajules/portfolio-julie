About

A storyteller who loves games. A gamer who loves storytelling.
Studying Games & Immersive Media in Germany, Julie has always had a keen interest in combining intricate stories with interactive media, immersing players fully into a world they can experience on their own.
Having been a writer since age five and a tabletop game master since age 14, she grew up with the medium of stories and games. During her youth, she honed her skills in writing, worldbuilding, and graphic design (as her horde of ideas all needed book covers).
Now, she learns how to design and develop games. In the future, she hopes to combine both of her skillsets to create digital and physical games that make players laugh, cry, and think beyond the screen.
(And yes, she reads WoW quest texts. Every. Single. One.)
